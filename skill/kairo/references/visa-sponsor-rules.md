# Visa and sponsor check

Use this when the job is in a country where my-facts `Visa needs` says the user **needs** a visa. If not needed, the visa flag is `not_needed` and you skip this file.

Visa rules and salary minimums change every year. **Never quote a threshold from memory.** Open the official page (web search is fine) and give the date you checked. If you cannot open it, say "not verified".

## Steps

1. **Read the JD.** The scan gives `sponsorship: yes / no / mixed / unknown` with a quote.
   - `no` (or "local candidates only", "must already hold a residence visa / right to work") → visa flag `no`, blocking.
   - `yes` → keep the quote as proof, then still do the country check below where one exists (NL, UK).
   - `mixed` → read both quotes and decide; if still unclear, `unknown`.
2. **Country check** (table below).
3. **Visa flag:** `yes` (proof found), `no` (JD or register says no), `unknown` (could not confirm), `not_needed`.
4. **Proof:** one line with the source: a short JD quote, or the register page and the date you checked. Never write "verified" without a source you opened today.
5. **Default for unknown:** when a visa is needed and the flag is still `unknown` after the check, the verdict is **Skip (visa not verified)**. Tell the user they can say "apply anyway" if they know the company sponsors (for example a recruiter told them).

## Country table

| Country             | What makes sponsorship real                                                                                                                                             | How to check                                                                                                                                                                                             | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Netherlands (NL)    | The employer must be an IND **recognised sponsor** for the highly skilled migrant (kennismigrant) route.                                                                | Search the IND public register: ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors (register "Regular labour and highly skilled migrants"). Search the legal name (often "<Brand> B.V."), not only the brand. | Found → `yes`. Searched and not found under the brand or legal name → flag `sponsor_not_found`, visa `unknown` (default Skip). Register could not be opened, but the JD itself says "recognised sponsor" → `yes` with proof "JD states it; IND register not verified", and tell the user to confirm with the recruiter. Salary must also meet the IND income requirement for the user's age group: check ind.nl "required amounts". |
| United Kingdom (GB) | Employer holds a sponsor licence for the Skilled Worker route.                                                                                                          | GOV.UK "Register of licensed sponsors: workers" (gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers).                                                                                  | Salary and occupation rules apply; check gov.uk "Skilled Worker visa".                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Germany (DE)        | EU Blue Card or skilled worker visa. The employer does not need a licence, but the job must meet the Blue Card salary threshold and the user needs a recognised degree. | make-it-in-germany.com (EU Blue Card page).                                                                                                                                                              | JD silent → `unknown`; check the posted salary against the current threshold.                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## What to tell the user

One line, plain words. Examples:

- "Visa: yes. Tulip Commerce is on the IND register (checked 2 Nov 2026)."
- "Visa: not verified. The JD does not mention sponsorship and I could not find the company on the IND register. Skip, unless you know they sponsor."
- "Visa: no. The JD says candidates must already hold a UAE residence visa."
