# Changelog

All notable changes to Kairo. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04

The first public release.

### Added

- **Job check:** paste a link or job text and get a fit score (0–100), an **Apply / Maybe / Skip** verdict, red flags and a visa check, with one clear next step.
- **Apply pack:** a tailored, ATS-friendly CV PDF, a short cover letter and form answers, built only from your own facts.
- **Truth check:** every number, company, title and date in the CV, cover letter and answers must exist in your facts before you see them.
- **CV formats for 9 countries:** India, Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, Poland, France, UAE, Singapore and the United Kingdom.
- **Setup in chat:** upload your CV, answer up to 10 short questions, including work preferences (relocation, contract roles, shift work). Kairo never assumes them.
- **Never guesses money:** if a salary ask or floor is missing, Kairo asks you.
- **Status tracking:** say "applied", "interview", "offer" or "rejected" and the status is saved with its date: in a chat tracker, or on the free dashboard.
- **Optional dashboard connector:** sign in with your email at kairo.rohansurve.in and connect Kairo in Claude (connector contract v1.2). Only public job data, scores, verdicts, red flags, visa flags and status dates are sent.
- **Model tips:** a one-line tip on when to use a fast model and when to use your strongest one.
